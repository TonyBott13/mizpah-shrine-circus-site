Your privacy

This is the website of the Mizpah Shrine Circus in Fort Wayne, Indiana. Here is what we collect, what we do with it, and how to reach us. We keep it short and plain.

What we collect

  • The contact form. If you send us a note, we get your name, your email and your message. It goes to tickets@mizpahshrinecircus.com so we can write back. The form is delivered by a service called FormSubmit, which handles the message and does not keep a copy for its own use.
  • Your language choice. If you switch the site to Spanish, your phone or computer remembers that choice. It stays on your device. We do not see it.
  • Nothing else. You do not need an account. We do not ask for your address, your card or your kids' names. We never sell your information.

Cookies and ads

We run ads on Facebook and Instagram so families in Fort Wayne hear about the circus. To see if those ads work, this site uses the Meta Pixel. It sets a cookie and tells Meta that a visit happened and whether you tapped a ticket button. It does not tell us who you are.

You can turn this off. In your Facebook account, go to Settings and then Ads and set your ad preferences. Your browser can also block cookies. The site still works without them.

Other sites we send you to

Tickets are sold by Ticketmaster and the Memorial Coliseum box office. When you buy, you are on their site and their privacy policy applies. Our fonts load from Google Fonts. Our social pages are on Facebook and Instagram. Each of those companies has its own policy.

Kids

This site is for parents and grown-ups who buy tickets. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child sent us something, email us and we will delete it.
