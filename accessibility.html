Accessibility · Last updated October 16, 2026 · draft for the Mizpah Shrine to review

Everyone is welcome

We want every family to enjoy the circus, and every visitor to be able to use this site.

At the show

  • Wheelchair-accessible seating is in the White and Yellow sections of the Memorial Coliseum. You can buy it online. Pick the accessible seating option when you check out.
  • Need help choosing seats? Call the circus office at (260) 234-2172 or the Coliseum ticket office at (260) 483-1111.
  • The Coliseum has accessible parking, entrances and restrooms. Entrance 6 is closest to the ticket office.

On this site

  • The site works with a keyboard and with screen readers. Every photo has a text description.
  • Text is large and high contrast, and the page is written in plain language. It is available in English and Spanish.
  • If your device asks for less motion, the moving parts of the page stay still.

Tell us if something is hard to use

Email tickets@mizpahshrinecircus.com or call (260) 234-2172. Tell us the page and what went wrong, and we will fix it.
