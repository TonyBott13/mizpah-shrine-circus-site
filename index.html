Good to know

Questions parents ask

How long is the show?

About 2 hours. There is a 15-minute break in the middle.

Do kids need a ticket?

Kids under 2 get in free. Kids over 2 pay the same price as adults.

How much is parking?

$8 per car. You pay at the booth when you drive into the Coliseum lot.

What is the Circus Fair?

A free fair in the Coliseum basement. Petting zoo, face painting, a moon bounce, pony rides and elephant rides. It runs one hour before and one hour after each show. Some rides cost a little.

Is there wheelchair seating?

Yes. It is in the White and Yellow sections. You can buy it online. Pick the wheelchair seating option when you check out. Need help? Call the circus office at (260) 234-2172.

Can I take pictures?

Yes. But no flash during the show. It can hurt the performers. No video at any time.

Any military or member discounts?

No. Everyone pays the same price.

Can I get a refund?

No. The money goes to the performers. The show goes on even if you cannot come.

What if there is a snowstorm?

The show goes on in any weather. It is never cancelled.
